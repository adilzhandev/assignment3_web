About us

Who we are and why we love Kazakhstan

Our story

Discover Kazakhstan was started in 2020 by a group of students from Almaty. We want to show the world how beautiful our country is.

Kazakhstan is the 9th largest country in the world, but many tourists still do not know about it.

Our values

  • Respect for nature and traditions
  • Safety on every trip
  • Support for local families

How we plan a trip

  1. You choose a tour.
  2. We prepare the route, transport and hotels.
  3. A guide meets you at the airport.
  4. You enjoy the trip!
